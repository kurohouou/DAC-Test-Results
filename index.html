Grading System

Ratings evaluate peak-to-peak IRE voltage accuracy (1 IRE = 7 mV for 700 mV RGB/YPbPr; 7.14 mV for 714 mV S-Video/Composite), RGB channel tracking, black-level linearity, and unit-to-unit consistency.

  • ★★★★★ Reference No more than 1 error > 15 mV, little to no separation of R/G/B mV values, no major % difference from reference (e.g. ≤ 5% off), and minimal variability across multiple units.
  • ★★★★ Great No more than 1 error > 25 mV and < 10 mV of RGB channel separation at 100 IRE.
  • ★★★ Good No errors > 35 mV and < 15 mV of RGB channel separation at 100 IRE.
  • ★★ Useable Errors > 35 mV without severe % curve collapse (e.g. runs hot at highlights without crushing shadow detail).
  • ★ Avoid Greatly impacts viewing experience negatively—major functional faults, severe black crush (< 50 IRE voltage drop typical of AG6200/AG6201), or extreme over-exposure.

Important Hardware Footnotes & Warnings

S-Curve SNES 240p Test Suite Gamma Note
The SNES core's output curve naturally tracks slightly lower (−2 to −6 mV) below 50 IRE and slightly higher (+8 to +18 mV) at 50 IRE and above compared to a pure PC gray ramp (MisterCast + HCFR).
